HOW IT WORKS

From market event to finished broadcast.
No hands on keyboards.

Four services run the newsroom — the Watcher finds the story, the Orchestrator builds the case file, the Agents write and gate the script, and the Manager watches everything. Scroll through one story's 47 seconds.

STAGE 01 · WATCH MANAGER · OBSERVING 

            

          

        


        
        

          

            01
            
The Watcher never blinks.

            
It scans Tier-1 wires around the clock and polls SEC EDGAR every five minutes, across 32,000+ companies on 53 exchanges. A new filing or a market-moving headline becomes a story candidate in seconds — deduplicated, classified, queued.

          

          

            02
            
The Orchestrator builds the case file.

            
Sector, financials, history, prior coverage — the story is enriched with everything a reporter would want on the desk, and checked against what has already aired so the feed never repeats itself.

          

          

            03
            
Every fact earns its place.

            
Claims are extracted with citations, cross-checked against the company's official EDGAR filings by an independent model, and every source link is validated. A fact that can't be verified doesn't get written.

          

          

            04
            
The right desk takes the story.

            
Earnings go to Gerardo. A supply-chain move goes to Liam. A lawsuit goes to Sophia. Five specialist reporters, each with a distinct beat and voice — routed automatically.

          

          

            05
            
Written for broadcast, word by word.

            
Seventy to ninety-five words — a forty-second read. Numbers written out the way an anchor says them. Every claim attributed to its source. No forecasts, no advice, no hype. House style, enforced by machine.

          

          

            06
            
Two gates. No mercy.

            
A 110-point editorial score and a three-layer content integrity engine — 24 rules deep. Fail either gate and the script is rewritten automatically; fail again and it goes to a human review queue. It never reaches your users.

          

          

            07
            
On air — in your app, in your brand.

            
An avatar reporter renders the broadcast with captions, in up to eight languages, delivered as MP4 and HLS through an embed or API. From market event to finished script: 47 seconds. To video in your feed: minutes.

CONTENT INTEGRITY

        
Trust isn't a feature here.
It's the architecture.

        
Everything below was designed for one room: the one where your risk team asks the hard questions.


        

          

            
The three-layer content integrity engine

            
    
              
  1. 
                L1
                
    Forbidden Phrases Scan
                
    Instant, deterministic pattern scan. Sixteen forbidden phrases — “buy”, “sell”, “opportunity”, “could”, “might” — plus forward-looking language and formatting violations. Zero tolerance, zero latency.
    
              
    2. 
              
  2. 
                L2
                
    AI Meaning Check
                
    An independent model reads the script against a 24-rule editorial rulebook — implied advice, missing balance, subtle manipulation. The things a pattern scan can't see.
    
              
    3. 
              
  3. 
                L3
                
    Tone & Balance Review
                
    A final whole-script judgment: Is it balanced? Could the tone sway a trading decision? Is material context missing?
    
              
    4. 
            

            

              
35/110minimum quality score

              
AND

              
60/100minimum integrity score

            

            
Fail either gate → automatic rewrite. Fail again → human review queue. Blocked scripts never reach your embed.

          


          

            
Every fact, verified twice

            
Scripts are written by one model and fact-checked by a different one — numbers, comparisons, and claims each checked explicitly. Numeric claims are cross-referenced against the company's official EDGAR filings before air.

          


          

            
Zero user data. Ever.

            
Delivery is stateless — StockerTV never sees who's watching. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking, no behavioral profiles. There is nothing to breach and nothing to disclose. This website works the same way.

          


          

            
A human is always reachable

            
Anything the gates flag routes to a review queue with a real person behind it — triaged in minutes, around the clock. Editorial control can also sit with your team: approve-before-air is a configuration, not a feature request.
